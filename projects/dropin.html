drop-in – README.md

Drop In

LIVE

Foam dart extraction. Loot the park, dodge the darts, reach a green gate to bank your tickets – get tagged and you drop everything. A real multiplayer shooter that runs in a browser tab, no install and no launcher.

dropin.fly.dev

problem

The extraction loop – carry loot you can lose, decide when to walk – is the tensest thing in games. It's also locked behind 80GB downloads, launchers, accounts, and a console. None of that is the fun part. The fun part is a park, a backpack, and the choice to push your luck one more container.

built

  • The whole loop in a browser tab. No install, no launcher, no account. Click DROP IN and you're in the park with other people in a couple of seconds.
  • Greed is the mechanic. Tickets in your backpack are worthless until you reach a green gate. Bank small and safe, or keep looting. Get tagged and the backpack hits the ground for whoever's watching.
  • Foam darts, not bullets. Same tension, none of the grimness – a park, snacks that heal you, and a Goo Gun whose pull drops a pillar of instant cover you can double-jump on top of.
  • Plays on whatever you're holding. Mouse and keyboard, a controller it auto-detects, or touch with a thumbstick and drag-to-look. On iPhone, Add to Home Screen strips Safari's chrome and it opens fullscreen.
  • Voice, maps, and your own HUD. Live voice chat in the park, a built-in map editor, and a HUD you can rearrange. Park bots toggle off when you want real players only.

status

Live and playable right now – desktop, phone, or controller, from a link. The odd one out in this directory on genre and the most on-thesis on method: a realtime 3D multiplayer game is the kind of thing that "needs a team and a year," so it was worth finding out how fast one person could put a playable one in front of people.

Distribution is the feature. Every install step you delete is players you keep.
